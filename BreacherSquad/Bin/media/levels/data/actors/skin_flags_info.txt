layerVisibilityMask number - each bit is 0 or 1 where least important bit is layerIdx 1
example: we have 6 layers and layer 1, 3 and 4 are visible in this skin => 1101 in binary is 13
!layerVisibilityMask should be specified with hands visible!
Skins have quick access layer indexes to the hands layers as we need to be able to hide and show them depending on weapon loadout
handR_layerMask="16" handL_layerMask="8" specify the layer bit for each hand where handR is the main hand, always on the right side of the body. 
handL is the secondary hand hidden when using 2 handed weapons or dual wielding
